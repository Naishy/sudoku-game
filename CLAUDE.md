# Grid Works

One of Tim's family Christmas games: this one is for Heidi, Tim's sister-in-law (Ed's wife, Rex and Marlowe's mum), from Canberra, just turned 50, who loves Sudoku (she's very good) and good design, and works for an art gallery. Mel loves Sudoku too, and the rest of the family should be able to play it as well. **Read the family games playbook first:** `GAMES.md` in [Naishy/family-games](https://github.com/Naishy/family-games/blob/main/GAMES.md) (attach that repo with `add_repo`). It covers the shared setup, the scoreboard, the Christmas card, pilot stats and the workflow rules.

Specific to this game:

- **Live** at https://gridworks.tequinox.net once deployed by Workers Builds on push to `main`. Until it's deployed and working, build on `main`; after that, **all changes go through PRs**. Bump `VERSION` in `public/sw.js` and `public/js/telemetry.js` with each change.
- The idea (Tim chose it): a really good daily Sudoku, where **every finished grid becomes a painting** for her collection ("Works"), with a gallery wall label. Every number is a colour from the day's palette; each level paints in its own way (`public/js/painting.js`). One puzzle a day at each level, the same for everyone; extra ones after.
- It must be at least as good to play as the Sudoku app she already uses: notes, tidy notes, matching-number highlights, undo, hints, big keys, nothing in the way. Calm: no red, no alarms, the clock can be hidden.
- **Four levels**, named like stages of a painting: Sketch (singles only, 36+ clues), Study (pairs, pointing), Composition (triples, X-Wing, quads), Masterpiece (XY-Wing, swordfish, XYZ-Wing). Sketch is for the less keen players; Masterpiece is for Heidi and Mel.
- The rules: `public/js/sudoku.js` (no DOM): a fast solver for uniqueness and a human solver that grades puzzles and gives the hints. Every puzzle in the bank (`public/js/puzzles.js`, made by `npm run puzzles`, seeded; don't edit by hand) has exactly one answer and needs its level's techniques; the tests check all of them. Days, levels and scoring are in `public/js/days.js` (tuning in `LEVEL_INFO` and `CFG`).
- Ziggy, Ed and Heidi's cat, gives the guide and the hints (`public/js/lines.js`, drawn in `public/js/ziggy.js`), by sitting by the part of the grid to look at: Tim wanted a neutral guide (not Nonna: mother-in-law dynamics). Ziggy doesn't talk; the words describe what Ziggy does. Friendly and matter-of-fact: where to look, never "you got it wrong". Ziggy is a brown tabby with a white chin, chest and paws (drawn from Tim's photo; she), shown whole, not in a circle, and only on the grid: she strolls on to sit beside each hint, and the hint bubble shows the words alone (Tim didn't want two Ziggys at once) (`public/js/walk.js`: never on the hint's squares, the answer's square, or a number in the target's row, column or box); add her to the family cast if another game wants her. Heidi has the last word on each finished work: warm and easy ("Thanks heaps", "darl", "You're my favourite").
- The look: gallery white, Fraunces for titles, DM Sans for text, Heidi's emerald for the player's numbers. Wall labels like a real gallery's ("Study in Emerald, 2026. Glazed tiles."). No real gallery's name or branding, and no real artist's name.
- Heidi comes from the family cast (`public/js/cast.js`, copied with `node ../family-games/cast/sync.mjs public/js/cast.js`). Don't edit cast.js here.
- Heidi has an iPhone: no vibration from the web except the hidden-switch tap (`haptics.js`, iOS 18+), and `carry.js` keeps her works when it's installed.
- Pilot stats: the `family-telemetry` D1 database (`702e60c9-d505-4499-b2b2-602f4313da3d`), shared by every family game. This game's rows have `game='grid-works'`: filter every query and clear-out by it, never a bare `DELETE FROM events`. Look at the data before changing the scoring, then check with `npm run balance` and `npm test`.
- Board id `grid-works` (points, higher is better: the level's points, scaled by speed, less a little for hints). Never change it or how a puzzle scores without asking Tim: see the playbook.
- The gift is signed "Love from Tim & Mel" (`public/js/gift.js`).
