Welcome to Grid Works

A new Sudoku every day at four levels, from a gentle Sketch to a fiendish Masterpiece. Every grid you finish becomes a painting for your collection.

How to play

  • Fill the grid so every row, column and 3×3 box has the numbers 1 to 9, once each.
  • Tap a square, then a number. Tap a number with no square chosen to see where it is already.
  • Notes pencil in the numbers a square might be. They tidy themselves as you go.
  • Hint: Ziggy the cat strolls over and sits by where to look next, and you're told why. Tap again to fill it in.
  • explains every method Ziggy uses, from the simplest to the fiendish, with an example of each.
  • Four levels every day, the same for everyone: Sketch, Study, Composition and Masterpiece.
  • Together: two phones, one grid. Each of you plays in your own colour; join with the four-letter code.
  • Every finished grid becomes a painting. The day's palette colours it; the level decides how it's painted.
  • Points come from the level and your time, less a little for each hint. No rush: slow still counts.
